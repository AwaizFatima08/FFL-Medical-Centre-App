Privacy Policy

Applies to the Sahulat mobile app and web app — a voluntary community welfare tool built to support the operations of FFL Medical Centre, Rahim Yar Khan.

● Last updated: September 12, 2026

Sahulat ("the app") is an independent project, maintained by Homi Labs in coordination with the medical centre, and is not a formal IT-department product of Fatima Fertilizer Company. Installing and using this app is entirely optional, and no one is required to use an official company email address to sign up. This policy explains what information Sahulat collects, why, and how it's protected. If anything here is unclear, contact details are at the bottom of this page.

01 Who can use this app

Anyone may install the app, but creating an active account requires admin approval. Approval is reviewed manually by Medical Centre staff before an account can be used.

02 What information we collect

Account information

Email address and password used for signing in. Your password is never visible to us — it's handled securely by our authentication provider, Firebase Authentication.

Identity information (collected at signup)

Full name, employee number, phone number, date of birth, gender, CNIC, marital status, smoking status.

Residence information

Housing type and house/room number, or city of residence for non-residents.

Family member information

Basic details of entitled dependents (spouse, children) that you choose to add to your profile, used to coordinate medical facilitation for your family.

Health-related information you choose to submit

Feedback about visits to the Medical Centre, including the purpose of your visit and any comments · a description of the patient's condition when you request an ambulance · annual fitness activity records, if you participate in that program · blood group and donor availability status, if you opt in to the Blood Donor Directory · chronic or long-term health conditions, if you choose to disclose them for a dependent — visible only to the Chief Medical Officer and Admin In-Charge, never to reception or general staff.

Children's vaccination records

If a child you've added as a dependent is vaccinated through the Medical Centre's nursing staff using the app, we keep a record of the vaccine given, the date, and any adverse reaction noted.

Trip and ambulance requests

The pickup and drop-off details you type in, the requested time, and a short description of the reason for the trip or the patient's condition.

Notification delivery token

A technical identifier your device generates so we can deliver in-app notifications to you. It is not linked to any advertising system.

On location: the app does not access or collect your device's GPS location, at any time, for any feature. Pickup and drop-off details are a short description you type yourself, not a location captured automatically from your device.

03 How we use this information

  • To coordinate ambulance dispatch, medical trips, and doctor appointments, using the pickup and visit details you provide
  • To route your feedback to the right department
  • To maintain the Blood Donor Directory for voluntary donor matching
  • To send you in-app notifications about approvals, appointments, and Medical Centre circulars
  • To generate internal reports for Medical Centre planning (e.g. fitness participation, population statistics) — these reports use aggregated data and are for internal administrative use only

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

04 Who can see your information

Access is role-based — each role sees only what it needs to do its job:

You
Always able to view and edit your own profile and family information.
Reception staff
For scheduling trips and ambulance requests.
Nurses
For coordinating vaccination records and family/dependent information.
Doctors, CMO & Admin In-Charge
For care coordination and account approval. Chronic/long-term health conditions are visible only to the CMO and Admin In-Charge.
Drivers
Only the pickup/drop details and patient information needed to complete a trip they've been assigned to.
Lab & Pharmacy staff
Only notifications relevant to their own department.

Your information is never shared outside the Medical Centre's operational staff, and never shared with any external company or third party.

05 Where your data is stored

All data is stored on Google Firebase (Firestore database and Firebase Authentication), hosted in Google's Asia South (Mumbai) region. Access to the underlying systems is restricted to authorized administrators.

06 How long we keep your data

Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active. If you'd like your account or data removed, contact us using the details below and we will action the request.

07 Information about children

If you add a child as a family member/dependent, that information — including any vaccination record kept once they're seen by the Medical Centre — is provided and managed by you (the account holder) on their behalf. Children do not create their own accounts or provide information directly.

08 Your choices

  • You can view and update most of your own profile and family information at any time within the app
  • Some fields (like CNIC) are locked after signup for record accuracy, and can only be corrected by contacting an admin
  • You can opt out of the Blood Donor Directory at any time

09 This app is not your official medical record

Sahulat is a facilitation and coordination tool. Your official clinical records are maintained separately through the Medical Centre's dedicated clinical records system, not through this app.

10 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
