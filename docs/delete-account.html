Delete Your Account

How to permanently delete your Sahulat account and the personal data linked to it.

01 Request deletion

Sahulat doesn't yet have a self-service delete button in the app — deletion requests are handled directly by our team to make sure the right account is removed. Email us from the address registered on your account:

homilabs.smc@gmail.com Subject: "Delete my Sahulat account"

02 What happens next

We confirm it's you

We verify the request comes from the email address on file for the account before making any change.

Your account and data are deleted

Your login, profile, and the personal information tied to your account are permanently removed from our systems.

We confirm by email

You'll get a reply once deletion is complete, typically within a few working days.

03 What gets deleted

Deleted

Your login credentials, profile, family member records you added, feedback you submitted, fitness and blood donor records, and trip/ambulance request history.

Partial deletion is also available

If you only want a specific piece of information removed (for example, a family member's record) rather than your whole account, mention that in your email instead — we can action partial requests the same way.

Note: this deletes your app account. Any care you've already received at the Medical Centre is recorded separately in its own clinical records system, which this request does not affect — see our Privacy Policy, section 9.
